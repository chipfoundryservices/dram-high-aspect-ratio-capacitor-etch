# Glossary: DRAM High-Aspect-Ratio Capacitor Etch

Terms are defined as they are used in this book. Chapter references point to the main discussion.

---

## A

**A (aspect ratio):** Depth over width, h/w. The reference hole is 91:1 at its average CD (2100/23). (Ch. 1)

**A_tot (total aspect ratio):** (h_mask + h)/w, including the remaining mask. About 128 at the end of R1. (Ch. 2.3.4, 3.3)

**A_on (onset aspect ratio):** Aspect ratio above which twist appears; ≈ 30 for R1, ≈ 35 for R2. (Ch. 11.1.3)

**Acceptance angle:** Largest ion angle that reaches the bottom without striking the wall; arctan(w/h_tot) edge to edge. 0.45° at the end of R1. (Ch. 3.3)

**ARDE (aspect-ratio-dependent etching):** Fall of the bottom rate with depth. ER = ER₀/(1 + kA) (+ k₂A² above ≈ 100:1). (Ch. 4)

**ARDE coefficient (k):** 0.016 for R1, 0.010 for R2 (w = 23 nm); k = (3/4)χ. (Ch. 4.2)

**Arrival delay:** Extra time a narrow hole needs to reach the stop; ≈ 5.2 s per nm of CD_avg deficit in R1. (Ch. 12.2)

---

## B

**B-ACL (boron-doped amorphous carbon):** Reference hard mask, 1.50 µm; effective selectivity 3.5 (R1), 5.0 (R2). (Ch. 2.3)

**BO (bottom open):** Final low-energy step that clears the bottom SiN stop and lands on W; 35 s in R1. (Ch. 12.3.2)

**Bottom CD:** Hole diameter at the pad; 19 nm reference, ≥ 16 nm required. (Ch. 1.6.2, 12.2.3)

**Bow:** Widening below the neck from reflected, broad, and deflected ions; 27.5 nm at 450 nm in R1. (Ch. 10)

**Bow growth rate (r_b):** Lateral recession of the bow after it forms; ≈ 0.22 nm/min per side. (Ch. 10.3.3)

**Bridging:** Breakage of the wall between two neighbouring holes; limit 10⁻¹⁰ per pair. Driven by outliers. (Ch. 10.5)

**Broad population:** The ≈ 45% of ions that collide in the sheath; σ ≈ 2°, lower energy; mostly lost to the upper walls. (Ch. 3.2.2)

---

## C

**CD_avg:** Area-weighted average CD of the profile; sets capacitance. 23.9 nm as etched, 23 nm after electrode. (Ch. 1.4.3)

**CD-SAXS:** Transmission X-ray scatterometry that measures the average profile vs depth. (Ch. 15.2, App. F)

**Chamber constants:** Per-chamber recipe offsets that match a fleet. (Ch. 9.6)

**χ (consumption-to-supply ratio):** Fraction of the reactive neutral supply consumed at the open surface; k = (3/4)χ. (Ch. 4.2.2)

**Clausing factor (K):** Transmission of a tube with diffuse, non-sticking walls; ≈ 4d/(3L). 0.010 at L/d = 128. (Ch. 4.1.1)

**Coburn–Winters model:** Γ_b/Γ_t = K/(K + β(1 − K)). (Ch. 4.1.2)

**Cryogenic etch:** Etch at −40 to −80 °C using adsorbed HF; R2. (Ch. 7)

**Cyclic sub-steps:** Alternating deposition and etch sub-steps of 1–4 s to control the bow. (Ch. 8.3)

---

## D

**D_θ (angle diffusion):** Twist random-walk coefficient; 2.4×10⁻⁹ rad²/nm (R1). (Ch. 11.2)

**DC superposition:** Negative DC on the upper electrode, producing a ballistic electron beam that reaches the hole bottom. (Ch. 6.4)

**Droop:** Fall of the wafer surface voltage during the negative phase of a pulse-shaped bias; ≈ 66 V/µs. (Ch. 6.2.2)

---

## E

**Edge lag:** Later arrival of edge holes at the stop; ≈ 10 s at 147 mm. (Ch. 9.2.3)

**Edge tilt:** Systematic outward lean of holes near the wafer edge from the bent sheath; ≤ 0.10° at 147 mm. (Ch. 11.3)

**Effective selectivity:** Mold removed divided by mask consumed, including facets and overetch. (Ch. 2.3)

**EOT:** Equivalent oxide thickness of the dielectric; stalled near 0.50 nm by leakage. (Ch. 1.1.2)

**Etch–liner–etch:** Interrupting the etch to deposit a thin ALD liner that freezes the upper profile. (Ch. 14.1)

**EWMA:** Exponentially weighted moving-average feedback; λ ≈ 0.3. (Ch. 15.4.2)

---

## F

**Facet:** Sloped corner of the mask opening that descends ≈ 1.6× faster than the flat mask; its margin is the etch's clock. (Ch. 13.1.2)

**Facet margin:** Time before the facet foot reaches 150 nm above the mold; ≈ 128 s after R1. (Ch. 13.1.2)

**Feature-scale model:** Monte Carlo transport + surface kinetics + profile evolution for one hole. (Ch. 15.1)

**Flake:** Large particle (> 1 µm) from walls or rings; a die killer. (Ch. 9.5)

---

## G

**G(h):** Depth-time function h + kh²/(2w); t = G/ER₀. (Ch. 4.3, 8.1)

**Gap-fill limit:** The wall at the bow must hold two dielectric layers (≈ 2 × 4.5 nm). (Ch. 10.4)

**Gouge:** Recess etched into the W pad; 1–4 nm in R1; ≤ 8 nm spec. (Ch. 12.3.2)

---

## H

**h_on:** Onset depth for twist; 690 nm in R1. (Ch. 11.1.3)

**Hexagonal distortion:** Faceting of holes on a honeycomb, with flats facing the neighbours. (Ch. 13.4.2)

**HV-SEM:** High-voltage SEM (30–60 keV BSE) that images hole bottoms through the mold. (Ch. 11.6, App. C.4)

---

## I

**IED / IEAD:** Ion energy (and angle) distribution. (Ch. 3.2, 6)

**Ion funnel:** Guidance of narrow ions to the bottom by grazing specular reflections. (Ch. 3.3.2)

---

## K

**k₂:** Quadratic ARDE coefficient; ≈ 2×10⁻⁵ for R1. (Ch. 4.4.2)

---

## L

**Late arrival:** Not-open mechanism in which a narrow hole reaches the stop after the overetch ends. (Ch. 12.2)

**Lithographic pre-compensation:** Radial shift of the hole pattern at the edge to cancel the mean tilt. (Ch. 11.3.3)

---

## M

**Mask as the clock:** The principle that mask (facet) margin, not mold, sets how long the etch may run. (Ch. 2.3.3, 4.5)

**Maximum depth:** Deepest hole a chemistry and mask can reach; R1 ≈ 2.5 µm (facet), R2 ≈ 3.25 µm. (Ch. 4.5)

---

## N

**Narrow population:** The ≈ 55% of ions that cross the sheath without collision; σ ≈ 0.2°. (Ch. 3.2.2)

**Neck:** Narrowing just below the mask from polymer; 25 nm at 120 nm. (Ch. 10.2)

**Not-open budget:** ≤ 5 per die, 2×10⁻¹⁰ per hole. (Ch. 1.6.2, 12.4)

---

## P

**Pillar:** Free-standing TiN electrode after mold removal; 91:1. (Ch. 2.2)

**Placement budget:** RSS of overlay, mask open, tilt, and twist at the hole bottom; ≤ 7 nm. (Ch. 1.5, 11.4)

**Polymerization index (Π):** (C − O_feed − O_wafer)/F. (App. B.6)

**Pulsing (bias):** On/off modulation of the bias (5 kHz, duty 0.85) for charge relief. (Ch. 6.3)

---

## R

**R1:** Reference fluorocarbon etch at +10 °C, 20 kW tailored bias; 359 s. (Preface, Ch. 8.2)

**R2:** Reference cryogenic HF-based etch at −60 °C; ≈ 190 s. (Preface, Ch. 7, 8.2.3)

**Random walk in angle:** Twist model giving σ_x ∝ (h − h_on)^1.5. (Ch. 11.2.1)

**Reduced-order model:** The closed-form relations of this book used inside APC. (Ch. 15.1.4)

---

## S

**Salt residue:** (NH₄)₂SiF₆ formed in R2 on nitride; sublimed by post-etch warm-up. (Ch. 7.2.4)

**Sensitized monitor:** Wafer etched with a shortened overetch to raise the not-open rate to a measurable level. (Ch. 12.5.2, App. C.6)

**Side punch:** Etch into the pad isolation where the hole overhangs the pad; ≈ 21 nm deep in R1. (Ch. 12.3.4)

**Striation:** Vertical grooves in the hole wall transferred from mask roughness. (Ch. 13.3)

**Supports:** SiN layers (top 100 nm, middle 40 nm) that hold the pillars after mold removal. (Ch. 2.1, 2.2)

---

## T

**Tailored waveform:** Pulse-shaped bias that holds the sheath near −V₀ for most of the period. (Ch. 6.2)

**Tilt:** Systematic lean of holes, mainly at the wafer edge. (Ch. 11.3)

**Twist:** Random hole-to-hole wander of the bottom; 3σ 4.5 nm (R1), 3.0 nm (R2) at 2.1 µm. (Ch. 11)

**Two-tier mold:** Mold built and etched in two ≈ 1.05 µm tiers with a junction alignment. (Ch. 14.2)

---

## V

**VC (voltage contrast) inspection:** E-beam inspection that distinguishes open from not-open holes. (Ch. 12.5.1)

**Virtual metrology:** Prediction of post-etch results from sensors and incoming data. (Ch. 15.4.4)

**Void (electrode):** Unfilled region in the TiN pillar below a neck narrower than the bow. (Ch. 16.2.2)

---

## W

**WAC (waferless autoclean):** O₂/NF₃ plasma after every wafer to reset the walls; 60 s. (Ch. 9.3.2)

**Wall (between holes):** p − CD; 11 nm at the top, 9.5 nm at the bow; ≥ 9 nm required. (Ch. 1.3.2, 10.4)

**Wall loss (s_w):** Reaction probability of radicals on polymer-coated walls; adds attenuation exp(−A/A_w). (Ch. 4.1.3)
